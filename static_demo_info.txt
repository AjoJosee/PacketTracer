5. STEP-BY-STEP TESTING & VERIFICATION GUIDE IN PACKET TRACER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the .pkt file in Cisco Packet Tracer.
2. CRITICAL - BYPASS INITIAL CONVERGENCE:
   - When Packet Tracer first opens, switch ports take 30-50s to transition from STP listening
     to forwarding (amber dots turn green), and OSPF routers take ~40s to elect DR/BDR.
   - Click the 'Fast Forward Time' button (>>) in the bottom toolbar 2 to 3 times (or press Alt+D).
   - Verify all link lights turn solid green.
3. TESTING CONNECTIVITY:
   - Click on any PC (e.g. PC1) -> 'Desktop' tab -> 'Command Prompt'.
   - To test end-to-end ping from PC1 to PC2:
       ping 192.168.0.10
   - Note: The first ping packet may timeout due to ARP discovery; packets 2-4 will reply 100%.
